# Candidate Employability Analysis — Tech Recruitment

## Overview
A Power BI dashboard analyzing what actually drives hiring outcomes for tech job applicants — built to identify which candidate factors are the strongest predictors of getting hired.

## Data Source
73,462 tech job applicant records from the Stack Overflow Developer Survey (via Kaggle's "70k+ Job Applicants Data" dataset). Includes education level, years of coding experience, technical skill count, previous salary, country, and hiring outcome.

## Key Findings
- **Technical skills are the strongest hiring driver**: candidates with 21+ skills were hired at 96.6%, vs. just 1.7% for those with 5 or fewer.
- **Education level shows a counterintuitive pattern**: No Higher Ed (58.9%) and Undergraduate (56.0%) applicants had higher hire rates than Master's (48.6%) or PhD (28.6%) holders.
- **Market comparison**: hire rates and applicant volume vary notably across top markets (US, Germany, India, UK, Canada).

## Tools Used
Power BI (KPI cards, DAX measures, donut/bar/line charts), Kaggle
